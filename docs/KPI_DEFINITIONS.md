# CartCompass-BI — Key Performance Indicator (KPI) Definitions & Methodology

This document defines the mathematical formulas, business rules, filtering policies, data integrity constraints, and known limitations used throughout **CartCompass-BI**.

---

## 1. Executive Summary & Philosophy

**CartCompass-BI** is a management-oriented executive business intelligence dashboard. It focuses on **aggregate commercial health**, realized cash flows, category dynamics, regional performance, and period-over-period momentum.

Unlike CRM or operational logistics systems, this platform deliberately abstracts away individual customer identities and transaction-level line items in favor of executive-level business analytics.

---

## 2. Metric Formulas and Mathematical Definitions

### 2.1 Gross Sales
- **Definition**: The total commercial value of all eligible products sold before applying promotional discounts or return refunds.
- **Formula**:
  $$\text{Gross Sales} = \sum (\text{Quantity} \times \text{Unit Price})$$
- **Eligibility**:
  - In default reporting (*Realized Sales*): Only orders with `order_status = 'delivered'`.
  - In *All Active* mode: Orders with `order_status IN ('delivered', 'shipped', 'pending')`.
  - **Cancelled orders** are strictly **excluded** from all sales calculations.
- **Data Types**: `REAL` currency value (rounded to 2 decimal places).

### 2.2 Discount Amount
- **Definition**: The aggregate currency value of promotional discounts applied to eligible line items.
- **Formula**:
  $$\text{Discount Amount} = \sum \text{Discount}$$
- **Business Rule**: Discounts are stored as direct currency values (e.g., ₹150.00 off), not percentage integers. Discounts are constrained such that:
  $$0 \le \text{Discount} \le (\text{Quantity} \times \text{Unit Price})$$
  A discount cannot exceed the line item's gross value.

### 2.3 Return Amount
- **Definition**: The aggregate currency value of product returns and refunds attributed to realized orders.
- **Formula**:
  $$\text{Return Amount} = \sum \text{Return Amount}$$
- **Business Rule**:
  - Product returns only apply to orders that have been successfully **delivered**. Orders that are `pending`, `shipped`, or `cancelled` cannot have return amounts.
  - Return amount is capped at the net paid value per item:
    $$0 \le \text{Return Amount} \le (\text{Gross Item Value} - \text{Discount})$$
  - Return amounts represent the actual cash value refunded. They are subtracted once from Gross Sales.

### 2.4 Net Sales (Realized Revenue)
- **Definition**: The finalized commercial revenue earned by the business after promotional deductions and return refunds.
- **Formula**:
  $$\text{Net Sales} = \text{Gross Sales} - \text{Discount Amount} - \text{Return Amount}$$
- **Sign Constraint**: Because discounts and returns are validated and capped at line-item level, Net Sales is guaranteed non-negative.

### 2.5 Total Orders
- **Definition**: The distinct count of eligible commercial orders placed within the selected timeframe.
- **Formula**:
  $$\text{Total Orders} = \text{COUNT}(\text{DISTINCT } \text{order\_id})$$
- **Business Rule**: An order containing multiple line items (e.g., 3 products in one checkout) is counted **exactly once**. Multi-item orders never inflate the order volume count.
- **Exclusion**: Cancelled orders are excluded from eligible order counts.

### 2.6 Average Order Value (AOV)
- **Definition**: The average realized net revenue generated per fulfilled order.
- **Formula**:
  $$\text{Average Order Value} = \begin{cases} \frac{\text{Net Sales}}{\text{Total Orders}}, & \text{if } \text{Total Orders} > 0 \\ 0.00, & \text{if } \text{Total Orders} = 0 \end{cases}$$
- **Edge Case**: If zero eligible orders exist within the selected filter range, AOV safely evaluates to `0.00` rather than triggering a division-by-zero exception.

### 2.7 Monthly Revenue Trend
- **Definition**: Net Sales aggregated by calendar month (`YYYY-MM`) based on the order's placed date (`order_date`).
- **Grouping**:
  $$\text{Monthly Net Sales} = \sum_{i \in \text{Month}} (\text{Gross}_i - \text{Discount}_i - \text{Return}_i)$$
- **Return Attribution**: Because transaction-level return receipt dates are often separate from order placement in e-commerce pipelines, returns are attributed to the originating `order_date` to maintain consistent cohort margin visibility.

### 2.8 Revenue by Product Category
- **Definition**: Total Net Sales and percentage contribution generated by each product catalog category (e.g., Electronics, Home & Kitchen, Fashion & Apparel, Beauty & Personal Care, Sports & Fitness).
- **Revenue Share Formula**:
  $$\text{Revenue Share (\%)} = \left(\frac{\text{Category Net Sales}}{\sum \text{Category Net Sales}}\right) \times 100$$

### 2.9 Regional Sales Performance
- **Definition**: Net Sales, order volume, and AOV aggregated across geographical delivery territories (North, South, East, West, Central).

### 2.10 Revenue Growth (%) and Order Growth (%)
- **Definition**: The percentage change in performance between the current selected period and an immediately preceding period of identical duration.
- **Formula**:
  $$\text{Revenue Growth (\%)} = \begin{cases} \left(\frac{\text{Current Net Sales} - \text{Prior Net Sales}}{\text{Prior Net Sales}}\right) \times 100, & \text{if } \text{Prior Net Sales} > 0 \\ \text{N/A (None)}, & \text{if } \text{Prior Net Sales} \le 0 \end{cases}$$
  $$\text{Order Growth (\%)} = \begin{cases} \left(\frac{\text{Current Orders} - \text{Prior Orders}}{\text{Prior Orders}}\right) \times 100, & \text{if } \text{Prior Orders} > 0 \\ \text{N/A (None)}, & \text{if } \text{Prior Orders} \le 0 \end{cases}$$
- **Zero-Denominator Handling**: If the previous baseline period has zero revenue or zero orders, the system outputs `None` (displayed in the dashboard as `N/A`) rather than `Infinity` or raising a fatal calculation error.

## 3. Order Status Lifecycle & Reporting Policies

Every order in the system possesses one of four distinct lifecycle statuses:

| Status | Definition | Default Realized Reporting | All Active Mode | Total Breakdown View |
| :--- | :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| **Delivered** | Order successfully fulfilled and received by customer. Realized cash flow. | **Included** | **Included** | **Included** |
| **Shipped** | Order processed, packed, and in transit via carrier. Pending delivery confirmation. | Excluded | **Included** | **Included** |
| **Pending** | Order received and awaiting payment clearance or warehouse picking. | Excluded | **Included** | **Included** |
| **Cancelled** | Order aborted by customer or merchant prior to delivery. No revenue realized. | **Excluded** | **Excluded** | **Included** |

### Why Realized Sales (Delivered) is the Default
In conservative managerial accounting and financial reporting, revenue should only be recognized when goods are delivered and the performance obligation is satisfied (matching GAAP/IFRS principles). Including pending or in-transit goods risks revenue restatements if delivery exceptions or transit damages occur.

CartCompass-BI provides an executive toggle allowing managers to view **All Active** pipeline orders when evaluating warehouse fulfillment velocity.

## 4. Handling of Missing Data and Edge Cases

1. **Missing or Corrupted Dates**: Handled strictly via ISO format (`YYYY-MM-DD`). The data cleaning pipeline (`analysis/clean_data.py`) automatically coerces invalid dates and drops corrupted timestamps before loading.
2. **Negative Values**: SQLite schema constraints (`CHECK(quantity > 0)`, `CHECK(unit_price >= 0)`, `CHECK(discount >= 0)`, `CHECK(return_amount >= 0)`) prevent negative raw entries.
3. **Empty Date Filters**: If a date filter yields zero orders, all aggregate functions return `0.0` or empty series rather than null exceptions.
4. **Referential Integrity**: SQLite foreign key constraints (`PRAGMA foreign_keys = ON;`) enforce that every `order_items` record maps to a valid `orders.order_id` and `products.product_id`.

---

## 5. Scope & Intentional Design Choices

### Why Customer Segmentation is Excluded
1. **Management Level Focus**: CartCompass-BI is architected as an executive management cockpit focused on high-level operational metrics: revenue momentum, regional expansion, product category profitability, and discounting efficiency.
2. **Privacy by Design**: The schema omits Personally Identifiable Information (PII) such as customer names, phone numbers, email addresses, and credit card credentials.
3. **Reproducibility**: The dataset is fully synthetic and generated via a seeded pseudorandom engine (`seed=42`). Synthetic customer personas often produce unconvincing RFM (Recency, Frequency, Monetary) distributions that distract from core business intelligence mechanics.
