Business Rules & KPI Mathematical Formulations

View docs/KPI_DEFINITIONS.md →

1. Gross Sales

$$\sum (\text{Quantity} \times \text{Unit Price})$$

Calculated on eligible items prior to promotional discounts and return refunds.

2. Net Sales

$$\text{Gross Sales} - \text{Discounts} - \text{Returns}$$

Reflects actual realized commercial inflow. Guaranteed non-negative per line item.

3. Total Orders

$$\text{Distinct Count}(\text{order\_id})$$

Orders with multiple line items are counted exactly once to avoid volume inflation.

4. Average Order Value (AOV)

$$\frac{\text{Net Sales}}{\text{Total Orders}}$$

Returns 0.0 safely if eligible order count is zero. Cancelled orders excluded.

5. Revenue Growth (%)

$$\frac{\text{Current Net Sales} - \text{Prior Net Sales}}{\text{Prior Net Sales}} \times 100$$

Identical duration baseline. If prior net sales is zero or null, reports "N/A" rather than infinity.

6. Fulfillment Eligibility

Delivered: Realized Cash Flows

Shipped & Pending: In-transit / fulfillment pipeline. Cancelled: Strictly omitted from revenue.
