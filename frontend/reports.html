KPI Formulations & Mathematical Methodologies

Standardized business logic rules enforced throughout the analytics engine

Read Full Methodology Document →

1. Gross Sales

∑ (Quantity × Unit Price)
Calculated on eligible items prior to promotional discounts and return refunds.

2. Net Sales (Realized)

Gross Sales − Discounts − Returns
Reflects actual realized commercial inflow. Guaranteed non-negative per line item.

3. Total Orders

Distinct Count (order_id)
Orders with multiple line items are counted exactly once to avoid volume inflation.

4. Average Order Value (AOV)

Net Sales Total Orders
Returns 0.0 safely if eligible order count is zero. Cancelled orders excluded.

5. Revenue Growth (%)

Current Net Sales − Prior Net Sales Prior Net Sales
× 100
Identical duration baseline. If prior net sales is zero or null, reports "N/A" rather than infinity.

6. Fulfillment Eligibility

• Delivered: Realized Cash Flows • Shipped & Pending: Pipeline • Cancelled: Excluded from Revenue
Delivered represents finalized sales. Cancelled orders are strictly omitted.
